About Me

The woman behind the costume

I'm Corena Gibson — a Los Angeles-based costume designer, fabricator, and makeup artist with two decades of credits spanning indie features, studio productions, shorts, and television.

My work ranges from building leather armour from scratch to full wardrobe supervision on union productions. I'm the owner and designer of Aneroc, a bespoke fashion and costume studio offering pattern making, textile design, and stylist services.

Every stitch tells a story. My job is to make sure that story is unforgettable on screen.
